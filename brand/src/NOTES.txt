Source for the brand pack.

mark.py        The mark's geometry: angle, offset, stroke weight, letter
               line, viewBox. Every asset and every drawing in the manual
               comes out of this file.
spec2.py       The manual's page template: 1280x800, the type scale, the
               divider, the lockup at its published ratio. Reads fonts.css
               (HafsUthmanic, base64) and InterVariable.woff2, neither of
               which is copied here; take them from public/fonts/.
build_a..g.py  The pages, in order. Each writes one m_*.html.
assemble.py    Joins them into manual.html.
pdf2.mjs       Prints manual.html to the PDF through Chromium.
mkassets.py    Emits assets/*.svg.
mkpng.py       Writes the raster sources and the proof sheet.
raster.mjs     Rasterises them.

manual.html    The manual, self contained with its fonts embedded. Open it
               in a browser; the PDF is printed from it.

The app screens in section 3 were drawn from the running app with its
practice competition loaded, not from memory. To check one, run the dev
server, load the sample competition and judge a reciter.

To change the mark: edit mark.py, then run mkassets.py, mkpng.py,
raster.mjs, the build_*.py files, assemble.py and pdf2.mjs, in that order.
Do not edit an SVG by hand.
