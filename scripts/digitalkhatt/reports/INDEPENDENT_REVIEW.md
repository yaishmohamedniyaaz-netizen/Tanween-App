## Sample ownership evidence

Local high-resolution PDF crops and the actual content spans support these explicit assignments:

- Page 6, `2.31.9`: span `ِـٔ͏` contains separate `kasra` and `hamzaabove.joined` shows. Kasra belongs to the preceding beh target; hamza belongs to the next target.
- Page 6, `2.33.1`: span `ٓـٔ͏` contains separate `maddahabove` and `hamzaabove.joined` shows. Their different Unicode offsets support different target owners.
- Page 2, `2.4.9`: span `ْـٔ͏` contains separate sukun and `hamzaabove.lamalef` shows. The lam and alif bodies are also separate shows. The sampled crop does not show a single indivisible lam-alif body.
- Page 25, `2.164.40`: inspected the crop supporting separate fatha/hamza marks in the multi-target span.
- Page 11, `2.72.3`: the detached hamza and its sukun are visibly separate from ra and its small alif. A singleton-limited source-group split is supported without changing either source string.
- Page 282, `17.7.11`: the source's small high waw and added maddah sit together over sin, with the following hamza/damma separate. Keeping both source glyphs under sin while omitting the extra maddah only from the alignment key is supported as source-host projection. It is not full-text equality.

These are sampled source-identity checks. They do not establish that every isolated outline can be colored without overlap, that every tatweel has the intended owner, or that the app's final rendering/tray has passed visual review.

## Findings raised during implementation

1. Initial glyph generator lacked app/rule checksums and accepted invalid page limits. The worker added protected-corpus and target-rule/tokenizer hashes, `1..604` validation, duplicate-ID checks and cross-checks against the stricter parity extraction. Optimized Python mode is explicitly rejected so assertions cannot silently disappear.
2. Initial coverage counted ambiguous multi-target spans as covered. The reports distinguished shared ownership from unambiguous coverage; explicit mark identity plus Unicode offsets resolve sampled cases without inventing outline splits.
3. Stop signs must not be treated as mistaken-letter ink. Initial `map_word` assigned `waqf.qaf`, `waqf.jeem` and `waqf.sad` to the preceding target solely because they share its text group. Reproductions: p293 `17.105.3` index 57, `17.107.5` index 281, and `17.110.5` index 637. The worker corrected this to `decorative-waqf-no-target`, with empty tids. Independently reran all five p293 waqf instances: each is decorative, and none leaves an app target unmapped. The 22 current word/glyph tests pass. Final all-page report counts still require the final rerun.
