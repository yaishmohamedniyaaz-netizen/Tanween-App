How the mistake would look

The letters sit side by side with no gaps: each round handle is the border between two letters, so moving it grows one letter and shrinks the next. The outer two handles are the word's right and left ends. Pick a letter (or press 1–9) to bring its two borders forward and see its bar in the preview. Click on the word to move the nearest border there. A focused handle moves 1 pixel with the arrow keys, 5 with Shift.
