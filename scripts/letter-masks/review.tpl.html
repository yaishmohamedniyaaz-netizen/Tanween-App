How the highlight would look

Pick a letter, then drag the two round handles to its right and left edges; the letter body between them becomes that letter, and the neighbouring letters get the rest. Use Brush for stacked letters the handles cannot separate. 1–9 pick a letter, R edges (arrow keys move a focused handle by one pixel), B brush, F whole piece (good for dots and harakat), E clear, Z undo, Enter approve. Grey ink is not given to any letter yet. Click a letter chip to see only that letter in the preview.
