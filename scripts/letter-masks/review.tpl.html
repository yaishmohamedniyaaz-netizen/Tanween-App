How the mistake would look

Pick a letter (or press 1–9), then drag the two round handles to its right and left edges. Click on the word to move the nearer handle there. A focused handle moves 1 pixel with the arrow keys, 5 with Shift. The coloured lines under the word show every letter's span, so gaps and overlaps are easy to see. A dashed chip means that letter has no span yet.
