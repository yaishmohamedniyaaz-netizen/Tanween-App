Real size on the desktop page

Hold and slide across the word to move the bar from letter to letter; tap to choose one. With Several mistakes on (or holding Shift), each tap adds or removes a bar, each in its own criterion colour. Random test marks 1–3 random letters with random criteria. Tap a letter, or press ← → or 1–9, to move the bar. The two large handles are the ends of the selected letter's bar; the small dots are the other borders. While you drag, the letters are lightly shaded so you can see the split, and the letters on both sides of the border are shown above it; when you let go, the view goes back to the app look. A focused handle moves 1 pixel with the arrow keys, 5 with Shift. Z undo, Enter approve.
